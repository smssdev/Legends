  3. For each text block, extract the numbers in its text with /\d+(\.\d+)?/g, and check each one against the cited_text of its citations; flag a number found in none of them. On this chapter’s answer, “8%” and “1 December” are in the association’s cited text, and nothing is flagged; change the sentence to 9% in practice.json and the check catches it. A report that says how it was made is easier to trust, and easier to correct.
