The error says that a RenderFlex has children with flex but its incoming height constraint is unbounded: the ListView gave the Column a maximum height of infinity, and Expanded means “take what is left of the maximum”. Either give the column a bounded height (a SizedBox(height: …) around it, or no ListView if the page does not need to scroll), or drop Expanded and let the children take their own sizes with mainAxisSize: MainAxisSize.min.

In the row without keys, the text stays in its position and appears under the other letter, because the text lives in the State of the field, which the element kept; the focus and the cursor stay there too. In the row with keys, the text, the cursor and the focus move with their tile. This is the most common real-life version of the keys problem: forms whose fields are added or removed.
  5. The picture is painted once again: it now has its own layer, and when the spinner repaints the parent’s layer, the picture’s layer is reused without painting it. Both placements protect the picture; the difference is what else they protect. A boundary around the thing that changes protects all its neighbours at once, which is usually best; a boundary around one expensive static thing protects only that one, and is the better choice when that thing is costly to paint and the changing part is spread across the screen.
