  3. Bit Notes collects nothing and shares nothing: the notes stay in the phone’s storage, and the app makes no network request. In Play’s form that is “No data collected”, and in Apple’s “Data Not Collected”. With a backup server, the notes become “App content: other user content” (and an email address if there are accounts), collected for app functionality, not shared, encrypted in transit; you must also offer a way to delete the account and its data, and say so in the privacy policy, as Chapter 12 warned about everything kept.
