# Policies

- Opening hours: 7:00 to 23:00 every day; Fridays from 13:00.
- Refunds: a drink that is wrong is remade or refunded at once, no questions asked.
- Allergies: the date cake contains nuts; ask the barista about any other allergen.
- Wi-Fi: free for customers; the password is on the receipt.
- Loyalty card: the tenth drink is free; cards work in every branch.
- Branches: Zamalek, Maadi and Heliopolis.
- Groups of more than ten people should book a day ahead by phone.
- Laptops are welcome all day except Friday afternoons.
- Pets on a lead are welcome on the terrace.
