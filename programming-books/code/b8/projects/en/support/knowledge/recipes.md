# Recipes for baristas

## Latte
- Cup: 350 ml.
- Espresso: 2 shots (36 g), extracted in 25 to 30 seconds.
- Milk: 250 ml, steamed to 65 C.
- Serve at once; a drink that waits more than two minutes is remade.

## Rose Latte
- Cup: 350 ml.
- Espresso: 2 shots (36 g), extracted in 25 to 30 seconds.
- Milk: 230 ml, steamed to 65 C.
- Add 20 ml rose syrup to the cup before the espresso.
- Serve at once; a drink that waits more than two minutes is remade.
