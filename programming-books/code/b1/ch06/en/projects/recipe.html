Grandma’s Lentil Soup

A warm, simple soup ready in 30 minutes. Serves four.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of red lentils
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 carrot, chopped
  • 1 teaspoon of cumin
  • 5 cups of water
  • Salt and lemon

Steps

  1. Wash the lentils.
  2. Cook the onion and carrot in a little oil for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the lentils, cumin and water, and boil for 20 minutes.
  4. Blend until smooth, add salt, and serve with lemon.
